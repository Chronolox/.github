# Contributing to Chronolox

Thank you for your interest in contributing to Chronolox!

Chronolox builds decentralized digital inheritance and time-locked cryptographic storage solutions that are secure for families, non-technical heirs, and enterprises.

## How to Contribute

We welcome contributions across several domains:

1. **Specifications & Architecture ([`specs`](https://github.com/Chronolox/specs) repository):**
   - Reviewing cryptographic proofs and protocol models.
   - Clarifying documentation, threat models, and architectural guides.
   - Identifying edge cases in time-lock recovery and enclave boundaries.

2. **Application Distributions & Verification ([`heir`](https://github.com/Chronolox/heir), [`cli`](https://github.com/Chronolox/cli), [`secret-maker`](https://github.com/Chronolox/secret-maker), [`relayer`](https://github.com/Chronolox/relayer)):**
   - Testing binary verification on various OS platforms (Linux, macOS Apple Silicon / Intel, Windows).
   - Reporting packaging or installation anomalies.
   - Verifying GPG signatures and hash ledgers.

3. **Community & Founding Roles:**
   - Testing beta software and submitting usability feedback: [chronolox.com/community](https://chronolox.com/community).
   - Legal, estate planning, and domain expertise.
   - Founding contributor applications: [chronolox.com/contributors](https://chronolox.com/contributors).

## Guidelines for Issues & Discussions

- **Security Vulnerabilities:** Do NOT post security issues in public issues. Follow [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md) and email `security@chronolox.com`.
- **Bug Reports:** Provide system details, OS version, application version, steps to reproduce, and terminal/log output.
- **Tone & Community:** Be respectful, constructive, and focused on making security accessible to non-technical families.

## Pull Requests

1. Keep pull requests focused on a single change or clarification.
2. Ensure markdown formatting and links are tested and valid.
3. Sign your commits if possible (`git commit -s`).
